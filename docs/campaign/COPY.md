## r/windowsapps

**Title**

ClutterDock — stacks on the tray so the taskbar can actually get smaller (free unsigned beta)

**Body**

I'm the developer.

ClutterDock is a tray launcher: apps, files, folders, and URLs in named stacks. Hotkey is Ctrl+Shift+D. The idea is you pin ClutterDock, put the rest in stacks, and unpin the junk so the taskbar shrinks.

Download the **setup** installer from https://clutterdock.com/#download — not the portable exe. Portable unpacks to TEMP and antivirus hates that.

It is not code-signed yet. SmartScreen and some AV (Bitdefender included) may flag it. That's on me, not on you. Source is public: https://github.com/rbakman-rgb/clutterdock

After install: right-click the ClutterDock icon → Pin to taskbar.

Mac version exists; this post is Windows.
