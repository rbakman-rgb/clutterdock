Privacy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Summary

ClutterDock is a local-first app. Your folders, history, and settings stay on your device. We do not require an account and we do not operate a cloud backend that stores your launcher data.

What we store on your device

Configuration such as folder contents, preferences, launch history, and (if you activate Pro) your license key are stored in local application support directories on macOS or Windows.

What we do not collect

ClutterDock does not include analytics SDKs, ad trackers, or forced cloud sync in the current builds. Opening external links (for example Buy Me a Coffee or the product website) is under your control.

Purchases & license keys

If you buy Pro through a third-party store, that store processes payment under its own privacy policy. License keys are validated offline on your device.

Website

This marketing site is static. Hosting (for example GitHub Pages) may log standard web requests such as IP address and user agent according to the host’s policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub or reach out via your purchase/support channel.
