Privacy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Summary

ClutterDock is a local-first app. Your folders, history, and settings stay on your device. We do not require an account and we do not operate a cloud backend that stores your launcher data.

What we store on your device

Configuration such as folder contents, preferences, launch history, and (if you activate Pro) your license key are stored in local application support directories on macOS or Windows (for example ~/Library/Application Support/ClutterDock/ on Mac).

What we do not collect

ClutterDock does not include analytics SDKs, ad trackers, or forced cloud sync in the current builds. Opening external links (for example a purchase page or this website) is under your control.

Purchases & license keys

If you buy Pro through a third-party store, that store processes payment under its own privacy policy. License keys are validated offline on your device.

Website

This marketing site is static and is served at clutterdock.com (with clutterdock.app redirecting there) via Cloudflare, our hosting provider. Hosting providers may log standard web requests (IP address, user agent) according to their policies.

Update checks

Optional in-app update checks may query a public releases feed (for example GitHub Releases) over HTTPS. That request goes to the host under their privacy policy — not to a ClutterDock account backend.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach out via your purchase or support channel once ClutterDock launches.
