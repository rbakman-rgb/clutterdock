A few good questions

Before you make room.

Does ClutterDock replace my Dock or taskbar?

No. It adds a compact launcher alongside the Dock on Mac or the tray on Windows. Your familiar desktop stays in place.

Can I use it for free?

Yes. The core launcher includes five stacks with twenty items each, search within a stack, keyboard shortcuts, Recents, and Running. Pro is optional.

What can I put in a stack?

Applications, files, folders, and website links. Keep the things you use together in one named stack.

How do updates work?

The app can check for new releases. On Mac, download the update and replace ClutterDock in Applications. Installed Windows copies can download updates in-app and restart to install. Your settings and Pro license carry forward. Read the install guide.

Do I need to provide an email address?

No email or account is needed to download or use the free app. Optional install registration lets you choose whether to share an email for release news. See what gets shared.

Which computers are supported?

Mac with macOS 14 or later, including Apple Silicon and Intel. Windows 10 or later, with a 64-bit Intel or AMD processor. The Mac and Windows interfaces are shown above.
