Pricing FAQ

Quick answers about Free, Pro, and licenses.

Is SlaveDock free forever?

Yes. The Free tier stays free with core launcher features. Pro is optional for power users who need unlimited stacks and advanced tools.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase. We may add optional cloud sync later; if we do, that would be a separate choice — not a forced conversion of Pro.

Does one Pro key work on Mac and Windows?

Yes. The same offline license format unlocks SlaveDock Pro on both platforms.

What’s the difference between Pro and Pro Multi?

Pro is a personal unlock. Pro Multi ($29) covers up to 5 devices — useful for home + work machines or a household under fair use.

How do I activate Pro?

After purchase you’ll get a key like SDPRO-XXXX-YYYY-ZZZZ. Open SlaveDock → Settings → Pro and paste it. No account required.

What happens when I hit Free limits?

You’ll see a clear upgrade prompt (for example, “Free includes 5 folders”). You can still launch everything already in your folders — we never lock your existing apps behind a paywall.

Can I tip instead of buying Pro?

Absolutely. Free users can support development via Buy Me a Coffee. Tips don’t unlock Pro features.

Ready for Pro?

Automated checkout is next. Until then: download Free, tip on Coffee if you like, and email/DM for a Pro key after payment — or use a key you already have in Settings → Pro.
